The launcher and its three shell screens

Sixteen artboards: the splash and the launcher at one, four and nine icons in both themes; wallet, notifications (list and empty) and profile in both themes; the icon-system sheet; six avatar marks; the bell badge at 1, 9 and 99+. Three tokens were added past the original set: a muted blue for the unread wash and a backing pair for the swipe-delete state. Everything else is as given.

ONE ICON

The wallet and LIVE carry it, and they are enough — but only because the live row is allowed to be taller when it is alone, adding the fire time. What is coming next is one line of type under the grid, not a card. Nothing is padded.

NINE ICONS

No folders and no most-recently-used float: a grid that moves under your thumb cannot be learned. Two editorial bands set by the product, and a filter field that appears at seven. Recency is LIVE's job, and LIVE is already above the grid.

GLASS · REVISION

Surfaces are now 62% translucent over an 8px backdrop blur — SwiftUI .ultraThinMaterial. The hairline, the 18pt icon radius and the flame orange are untouched; card radius has since gone to 12pt, and the ground carries a slight tonal wash so the translucency has something to work against. No gradient on a card.

TAB BAR · REVISION

Нүүр / Түрийвч / Профайл, glass over the scrolling content. It has one consequence worth naming: the launcher's wallet strip and the wallet tab were the same tap twice, and at nine icons there was no room for both, so the strip came off. The balance is now one tap away rather than visible on arrival — a trade against the brief's «visible before any app is opened». Apps stay icons; the bar carries only the shell.

ASSUMPTIONS

1pt = 1px here. SF Pro and SF Mono are not on the web, so Golos Text / JetBrains Mono stand in — both carry Cyrillic and are drawn for screen at these sizes. Bell, chevron and magnifier are drawn to SF Symbols' metrics.

AVATAR MARKS · SIX SEEDS · 54PT
{{ a.seed }}

Four rows of four cells, the right half mirrored, one cell per hex character. A character divisible by three leaves its cell empty; odd characters are round, even ones square; ink or ink2 for tone, and at most one cell takes the accent. Symmetry keeps it from reading as noise at 54pt; the empty cells are what make two accounts tellable apart.
